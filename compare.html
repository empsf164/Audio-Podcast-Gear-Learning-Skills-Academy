Acoustic & Hardware Comparison

Evaluate 2 to 4 devices side-by-side using empirical engineering benchmarks.

POLAR REJECTION

Cardioid patterns offer the highest rejection at 180° (directly behind the mic), making them ideal for home studios with wall reflections.

SAMPLE RATES

While 48 kHz is the international standard for podcasting and video, 96 kHz allows higher headroom when pitching or applying heavy spectral repair.

EIN NOISE FLOOR

Look for preamps with -128 dBu EIN or lower. This ensures pristine gain when amplifying quiet voices without introducing background hiss.
