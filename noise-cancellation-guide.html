ACOUSTIC RESEARCH LABORATORY

Understanding Noise in Your Recording Environment

Noise is not a curse—it is a physical wave propagating through air and building materials. In professional audio engineering, we treat noise through a rigorous four-stage hierarchy: Isolation, Decoupling, Polar Orientation, and Gating.

The Engineering Law of Noise Reduction: There is no digital algorithm or hardware capsule that can eliminate 100% of unwanted sound without creating phase distortion, unnatural frequency notches, or robotic vocal timbre.
NOISE TAXONOMY

The Three Noise Vector Categories

Before attempting to solve audio problems, you must identify whether your unwanted signal travels through air, solid matter, or wall reflections.

1. Environmental Noise

Airborne Sound Waves

Sound generated outside your microphone's nearfield zone: street traffic, air conditioning airflow, computer cooling fans, and nearby sirens.

  • HVAC ducts & window gaps
  • High-velocity GPU/CPU fans
  • Low-frequency city rumble

2. Mechanical Noise

Structure-Borne Vibrations

Physical kinetic energy traveling directly through physical contact: mechanical keyboard clatter, mouse clicks, and floor footfall footsteps.

  • Desk bumps into rigid mic stands
  • Spring squeaks on cheap boom arms
  • Typing shock through wood tabletops

3. Room Reflections

Acoustic Boundary Echo

Your voice reflecting off bare drywall, hardwood floors, and glass windows. The delayed reflections enter the mic out-of-phase with your direct voice.

  • Flutter echo & hollow resonance
  • Comb filtering cancelations
  • Muddy low-mid buildup (200–500 Hz)
ENGINEERING SOLUTIONS

Effective Control Methodologies

Ranked from highest audio quality preservation to highest processing tradeoff.

METHOD 01
Proximity & Placement

Moving your mouth to 2–3 inches from the capsule instantly boosts the Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) by over +12 dB.

METHOD 02
Cardioid Null Positioning

Orienting the 180° dead-zone rear of the mic directly toward computer fans or reflective walls drops unwanted bleed by 20 dB.

METHOD 03
Shockmount & Low-Profile Arm

Elastic suspension bands absorb mechanical vibrations before they reach the transducer capsule.

METHOD 04
Downward Expander / Gate

Gently mutes background room tone by -10 dB when you stop speaking, without abruptly chopping off natural breath endings.

INTERACTIVE LABORATORY

Audition High-Pass Rumble Elimination

Listen to synthetic air-conditioner low frequency pink noise rumble. Toggle the 80 Hz high-pass low-cut filter to observe how sub-bass energy vanishes while human vocal presence frequencies (1 kHz - 4 kHz) remain pristine.
